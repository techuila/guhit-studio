## 7. Aliteo's promise to you

For as long as Aliteo distributes the Project publicly, it will keep the Project's source code, including your Contributions, publicly available under an open source license approved by the Open Source Initiative. Aliteo may also offer the Project under other terms and may use it in products that are not open source, as section 2 allows.

## 8. How you agree

You agree to this agreement by following the link in the CLA bot's comment on your first pull request and confirming with your GitHub account. The bot records your GitHub username, the date, and the pull request. The agreement then covers your earlier and later Contributions to the Project, until you tell us in writing that it should stop covering new ones. Withdrawing does not affect Contributions already made.

We keep the information the bot records only to show that you agreed, and we do not share it except where the law requires.

## 9. Changes and the whole agreement

Aliteo may publish a new version of this agreement. A new version applies to Contributions you make after you agree to that version; Contributions made earlier stay under the version you agreed to then. This is the whole agreement between you and Aliteo about Contributions.
